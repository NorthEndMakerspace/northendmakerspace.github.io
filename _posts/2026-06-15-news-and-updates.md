## Come tour, teach, tweak, and tend

{% picture images/posts/2026-06-15-news-and-updates/volunteer-band-saw.png class="float-right-50" alt="A bearded man in gray shirt, jeans, clear plastic goggles and protective headphones feeds a small piece of wood into a large blue band-saw." %}

North End Makers works smoothly thanks to the busy volunteers who keep it humming.

We would **love to have you** join the ranks of excellent NEM members who welcome new folks into the makerspace, train them how to use complicated and potentially dangerous tools safely and successfully, and keep our workshops well-tuned for smooth operation.

NEM is recruiting folks who can commit 8 hours a month to being:

- **Tour Guides** with enthusiasm for welcoming potential new members
- **Instructors** to train members on 2D, 3D, woodworking, electronics, fiber arts, and more
- **Stewards** to maintain and troubleshoot our tools and keep workshops safe and clean

## Our windows are being watched

{% picture images/posts/2026-06-15-news-and-updates/window-warden.png class="float-right-50" alt="A small LCD message panel beside a door with a label that reads LAST ONE OUT MUST LATCH WINDOWS SHUT." %}

To safeguard all of NEM's tools and other important workspace contents, Michael T. programmed and installed a "window warden" by the front door to keep track of which windows are being left open - and to help the last member out to remember to close and latch them.

He adds, "Let me know if there's any issues & feel free to just unplug it if it's misreporting/causing issues!"
