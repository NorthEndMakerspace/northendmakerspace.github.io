## We Refreshed Our Makerspace Policies

What are rules for? NEM's policies and guidelines keep our makerspace safe and functional for everyone, at a physical, procedural and personal level.

Last week, the NEM Board and our various teams just finished a major write-thru of all polices, covering everything from tool use and safety gear to PETG and pets.

Please [take a look at our policies](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1HptOQzgVZ-yu8OgSVEGDbhEkyyjTt8Uxj9AuW-ICCJk/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.l2oyg1630r02). If you have any questions or concerns, you can reach the board at [board@northendmakers.org](mailto:board@northendmakers.org) via email or [@board of directors](https://discord.com/channels/775417143029661748/775417143616602145/1503057435412267129) on Discord.

## Announcing Online Training

We are building our training courses online to improve how well and quickly you can learn skills with NEM's tools and earn your certification to start using them.

Log into your Wild Apricot account and check out [the courses](https://learn.northendmakers.org) for:

- [CNC Machining](https://learn.northendmakers.org/course/view.php?id=3)
- [Laser Cutting and Engraving](https://learn.northendmakers.org/course/view.php?id=2)
- Waterjet Cutting (coming soon)

Meantime, the Facilities Team has cured some bugs with the CNC toolset.

Michael T reports: "Fixed the longstanding reliability issue we've had with the Z-axis. Re-wired all limit switches to be normally-closed so any future issues should trip an alarm rather than gouge your work (or destroy the machine!). We're planning on continuing to clean up the wiring in the near future. Thank you @chapatt and @ptan for your help!"

## Your Robot Friend

We now have a Discord bot! It currently does two things:

- manages some roles (`member` and all of the steward roles)
- gives you the `/certs` slash command to see which certs you have

You must put your Discord **username** into Wild Apricot for this to work! [https://nem.wildapricot.org/Sys/Profile](https://nem.wildapricot.org/Sys/Profile)

- click "Edit profile"
- scroll down to "Discord Username"
- fill it out and hit Save

Please note: it will take up to 15 minutes after you do this for the bot to recognize you

## Volunteer at NEM - All kinds of help needed

Our Volunteering Team has been busy, and we welcome your help and ideas.

We're working on:

- Recruiting more shop stewards
- Setting up a volunteer form available to members
- Training new NEM tour guides
- Connecting makers who want to share skills to collaborate on bigger projects
- Inviting members to offer their skills to greater Seattle
- Hearing from YOU about how you'd like to help lend your time and wisdom to NEM's expanded community.

If you see yourself fitting in anywhere in these topics, hit us up in the [Suggestion Box](https://discord.com/channels/775417143029661748/1500989514762551466) on Discord.

**We want to hear from you.**
