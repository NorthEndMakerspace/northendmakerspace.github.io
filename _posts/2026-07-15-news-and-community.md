## How We Keep NEM Safe

The North End Makerspace works smoothly thanks to our culture and our people. We all agree to collaborate, to teach each other, and to keep each other (and our marvelous tools) safe from harm.

We want our members to know that if they make mistakes, it's okay so long as they tell us – so that they can learn from what happened and avoid repeating the behavior. 

We have begun work to update our [Code of Conduct]({{ 'conduct' | relative_url }}) and the [Makerspace Policies](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1HptOQzgVZ-yu8OgSVEGDbhEkyyjTt8Uxj9AuW-ICCJk/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.l2oyg1630r02) that govern our community and protect the individual workshops by establishing clear consequences for people who knowingly cause harm.

We are forming a members' committee to review these policies, manage harmful situations, and codify consequences for specific acts.

Please reach out to [board@northendmakers.org](mailto:board@northendmakers.org) or [volunteer@northendmakers.org](mailto:volunteer@northendmakers.org) if you would like to join this work.

## You Might Need - or BE - a Peer-to-Peer Volunteer ...

{% picture images/posts/2026-07-15-news-and-community/maker-profiles.png class="float-right-50" alt="Maker Profiles example" %}

NEM always welcomes our members to volunteer. We are actively recruiting folks who can [commit 8 hours a month](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScQOkdR1qrkLoF1_vPvnCEnkKxxAXFM7VBwXSe1iUTGFr06mQ/viewform) to being:

- **Tour Guides** with enthusiasm for welcoming potential new members
- **Instructors** to train members on 2D, 3D, woodworking, electronics, fiber arts, and more
- **Stewards** to maintain and troubleshoot our tools and keep workshops safe and clean

But if you can't commit that time and you're still game to help your fellow members - there's another way:

Create a [Maker Profile](https://discord.com/channels/775417143029661748/1484988161456210120) so they can reach out to you: Use the template in the Maker Profiles channel on Discord to describe the skills, tools, and time you'd like to share. 

And if you're on the other side of the skillset - as a member needing help or seeking someone to show you the ropes on a particular technique - Browse the [Maker Profiles here](https://discord.com/channels/775417143029661748/1484988161456210120).

## Please Don't "Fix" Our Tools

No, seriously: If you think a tool is broken, do NOT try to repair it; NEM members are not allowed to adjust, calibrate, or otherwise work on the tools, unless expressly told to do so by the stewards or in the certification classes.

For example, on numerous occasions, something has gone wrong with our 3D printers, and a member tried to fix them - and only wound up making things worse.

If you're having a problem with a tool, please report it to @steward in the right channel in Discord.
